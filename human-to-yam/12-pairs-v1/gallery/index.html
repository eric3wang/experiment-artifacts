What this demonstrates: Human task references → manually authored geometry and waypoints → YAM inverse kinematics → recorded Isaac physics. This is an assisted prototype, not automatic video reconstruction or a learned policy. Scale, camera and materials are approximate. Rigid grasps use contact; cloth/cable use idealized attachments. Cloth robot collisions are disabled. Cable connector fit is unvalidated. A partial or failed attempt stays visible.

Playback: Selected human intervals and simulated actions are stretched to the same 18-second comparison cycle; intermediate events are not precisely aligned. Original files are preserved; H.264 playback copies are made for browser compatibility. Human footage: EgoDex / Apple AIML Research, dataset CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
