What this demonstration proves

These are manually reconstructed scenes with scripted robot waypoints. Objects are scaled to YAM’s reachable workspace; this is a task-level reproduction, not a metrically calibrated digital twin or a learned video-to-policy system. Each result is one recorded trial, not a benchmark success rate. The packing example reproduces the first block of the longer human sequence.

Isaac runs at 120 physics steps per second. All three rigid-object examples use finger contact without grasp attachments or object teleporting. Smooth visible mug and bowl geometry uses simplified compound collision shapes. Videos are retimed for comparison; the Isaac playback is 2.25× real time.

The human references are streamed from H2RBench, credited to its authors. Our YAM simulations were generated separately and are not H2RBench robot results. Recorded results and provenance · Download the YAM-only reel
